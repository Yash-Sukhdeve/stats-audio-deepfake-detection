PAC-Bayes Theorem Fundamentals\n\n### What is a PAC-Bayesian Bound?\n\nA PAC-Bayesian bound is a **high-probability generalization guarantee** that holds uniformly over all posterior distributions Q on a hypothesis space, given a prior P chosen before seeing data. The core mechanism is a **change-of-measure inequality**: it converts the difficulty of controlling the risk of every individual hypothesis (which requires union bounds over potentially uncountable classes) into controlling the expected risk under a distribution Q, at the cost of a penalty term measuring how far Q deviates from P.\n\nThe fundamental insight: if Q is \"close\" to P (in KL divergence), then the expected population risk under Q cannot be much larger than the expected empirical risk under Q. The KL divergence acts as a **complexity measure** -- it penalizes posteriors that concentrate on hypotheses the prior considered unlikely.\n\n**Why KL(Q||P) appears.** The proof uses Donsker-Varadhan's variational representation of KL divergence. For any measurable function f and distributions Q, P:\n\n```\nE_{T~Q}[f(T)] <= KL(Q||P) + ln(E_{T~P}[exp(f(T))])\n```\n\nThis is the change-of-measure inequality. Setting f(T) = m * (L_D(h_T) - L_hat_S(h_T))^2 and applying a moment-generating function bound on the right-hand side yields the PAC-Bayes bound. The KL divergence is the \"price\" of switching from the prior measure to the posterior measure.\n\n### McAllester (1999) vs Catoni (2007)\n\n**McAllester's original form** (McAllester, 1999, DOI: not assigned; COLT proceedings):\n\nWith probability >= 1 - delta over S ~ D^m, for all Q simultaneously:\n\n```\nE_{T~Q}[L_D(h_T)] <= E_{T~Q}[L_hat_S(h_T)] + sqrt( [KL(Q||P) + ln(2*sqrt(m)/delta)] / (2m) )\n```\n\nThis uses the sub-Gaussian concentration approach. The sqrt(m) inside the log comes from a specific union bound trick over discretized risk values.\n\n**Catoni's form** (Catoni, 2007, *PAC-Bayesian Supervised Classification*, Lecture Notes in Statistics 163, Springer, DOI: 10.1007/978-3-540-72927-3):\n\nFor any lambda > 0, with probability >= 1 - delta, for all Q:\n\n```\nE_{T~Q}[L_D(h_T)] <= (1 - exp(-C)) / C * E_{T~Q}[L_hat_S(h_T)] + [KL(Q||P) + ln(1/delta)] / (C * m)\n```\n\nwhere C = lambda / m.
